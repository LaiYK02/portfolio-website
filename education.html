EDUCATION JOURNEY

My academic journey from primary school to university has shaped my passion for technology, creativity, and game development.

SJK(C) Sin Min Kuala Sawah

2011 - 2016

I started my primary school journey at SJK(C) Sin Min Kuala Sawah in 2011. Although the school had only a small number of teachers and students, the teachers were hardworking and dedicated to helping us succeed.

During my time there, I made many good friends and built valuable memories. After achieving excellent UPSR results in Standard 6, the school rewarded me with a bicycle as a special gift.

SMK Rantau

2017 - 2024

I continued my studies at SMK Rantau in 2017. During Form 4 and Form 5, I took the Economy and Additional Mathematics stream.

I achieved 7A and 2B in SPM. I also met many close friends there, and some of us still keep in touch today.

After SPM, I continued my Form 6 studies at the same school. Since the school mainly offered Economy and History streams, I decided to continue with Economy even though I was highly interested in IT subjects at the time.

Eventually, I graduated from STPM with a CGPA of 3.67 / 4.00.

Form 6 Graduation

These graduation photos were taken near the end of my Form 6 journey. It represents an important milestone in my academic life before continuing my university studies.

Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka
(UTeM)

2025 - Present

In 2025, I began my degree studies at Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka (UTeM).

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology (Game Technology) with Honours.

Since I have always been passionate about games and technology, Game Technology became my first choice. I am happy that the university offers many IT-related subjects that allow me to continuously improve my programming and development skills.

Besides academics, I have also made many new friends and gained valuable experiences throughout my university journey.
